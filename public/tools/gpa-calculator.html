About GPA Calculator

Weighted and cumulative GPA on the standard 4.0 scale, with credit hours. Press Calculate to see the result in the results panel. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Only the current result is shown — no history or side-by-side comparison.

GPA Calculator reads "Previous GPA (optional)" and "Previous credits" from the form above and writes the answer into the results panel. There is no account, no upload and no server round-trip: the JavaScript on this page does the work on your own device.

How to use GPA Calculator

  1. Open GPA Calculator from Health & Everyday, or find it with the search box in the header.
  2. Fill in the fields shown above: "Previous GPA (optional)" and "Previous credits".
  3. Press Calculate. The result replaces whatever was shown before; only the current run is displayed, never two results side by side.
  4. Use Copy to put the result on your clipboard. A message appears only when something was actually copied.

A worked example

Enter your own values in the fields above and press Calculate. Change any input and press again; the result is recalculated from the current inputs and replaces the previous one.

Frequently asked questions

Is what I type uploaded anywhere?

No. GPA Calculator processes "Previous GPA (optional)" and "Previous credits" with JavaScript inside your browser. Your inputs and the result stay on your device; Utilixo keeps no copy of them.

Can I keep several results to compare?

No. Each press of Calculate rebuilds the result from the current inputs and overwrites the previous one. To compare runs, note the earlier result down yourself.

Anything specific to watch with GPA Calculator?

The result depends only on the fields shown above ("Previous GPA (optional)" and "Previous credits"); change one of them and press Calculate again to get an updated answer.

How do I take the result away?

Press Copy after a result appears. The copy button only reports success when there is content to copy.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. GPA Calculator is free, needs no registration and has no usage limit. Open the page and use it.
