About Favicon Generator

Turn a square image into favicon sizes plus the HTML tags to install them. Press Generate favicon to see the result in the results panel. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Only the current result is shown — no history or side-by-side comparison.

Favicon Generator reads “Choose a square image (256×256 or larger)” from the form above and writes the answer into the results panel. There is no account, no upload and no server round-trip: the JavaScript on this page does the work on your own device.

How to use Favicon Generator

  1. Open Favicon Generator from Generators & Utilities, or find it with the search box in the header.
  2. Fill in the fields shown above: “Choose a square image (256×256 or larger)”.
  3. Press Generate favicon. The result replaces whatever was shown before; only the current run is displayed, never two results side by side.
  4. Use Copy tags to put the result on your clipboard. A message appears only when something was actually copied.

A worked example

Enter your own values in the fields above and press Generate favicon. Change any input and press again; the result is recalculated from the current inputs and replaces the previous one.

Frequently asked questions

Is what I type uploaded anywhere?

No. Favicon Generator processes “Choose a square image (256×256 or larger)” with JavaScript inside your browser. Your inputs and the result stay on your device; Utilixo keeps no copy of them.

Can I keep several results to compare?

No. Each press of Generate favicon rebuilds the result from the current inputs and overwrites the previous one. To compare runs, note the earlier result down yourself.

Anything specific to watch with Favicon Generator?

The result depends only on the fields shown above (“Choose a square image (256×256 or larger)”); change one of them and press Generate favicon again to get an updated answer.

How do I take the result away?

Press Copy tags after a result appears. The copy button only reports success when there is content to copy.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. Favicon Generator is free, needs no registration and has no usage limit. Open the page and use it.
