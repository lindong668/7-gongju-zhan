About QR Code Generator

Turn any text or link into a QR code, rendered locally and downloadable as PNG. Press Generate to see the result in the results panel. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Only the current result is shown — no history or side-by-side comparison.

QR Code Generator reads "Text or URL", "Size" and "Error correction" from the form above and writes the answer into the results panel. There is no account, no upload and no server round-trip: the JavaScript on this page does the work on your own device.

How to use QR Code Generator

  1. Open QR Code Generator from Generators & Utilities, or find it with the search box in the header.
  2. Fill in the fields shown above: "Text or URL", "Size" and "Error correction".
  3. Press Generate. The result replaces whatever was shown before; only the current run is displayed, never two results side by side.

A worked example

Enter your own values in the fields above and press Generate. Change any input and press again; the result is recalculated from the current inputs and replaces the previous one.

Frequently asked questions

Is what I type uploaded anywhere?

No. QR Code Generator processes "Text or URL", "Size" and "Error correction" with JavaScript inside your browser. Your inputs and the result stay on your device; Utilixo keeps no copy of them.

Can I keep several results to compare?

No. Each press of Generate rebuilds the result from the current inputs and overwrites the previous one. To compare runs, note the earlier result down yourself.

Anything specific to watch with QR Code Generator?

The result depends only on the fields shown above ("Text or URL", "Size" and "Error correction"); change one of them and press Generate again to get an updated answer.

How do I take the result away?

Read the value in the results panel; this page has no copy or download button.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. QR Code Generator is free, needs no registration and has no usage limit. Open the page and use it.
