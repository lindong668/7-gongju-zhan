About Sleep Cycle Calculator

Bedtimes and wake times aligned to 90-minute sleep cycles, with the 15-minute fall-asleep buffer. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Only the current result is shown — no history or side-by-side comparison.

Sleep Cycle Calculator reads "Wake-up time", "Minutes to fall asleep" and "Sleep cycles (×90 min)" from the form above and writes the answer into the results panel — A sleep cycle averages about 90 minutes. Waking at the end of a cycle usually feels easier than waking mid-cycle. There is no account, no upload and no server round-trip: the JavaScript on this page does the work on your own device.

How to use Sleep Cycle Calculator

  1. Open Sleep Cycle Calculator from Health & Everyday, or find it with the search box in the header.
  2. Fill in the fields shown above: "Wake-up time", "Minutes to fall asleep" and "Sleep cycles (×90 min)".
  3. Press Calculate. The result replaces whatever was shown before; only the current run is displayed, never two results side by side.
  4. Use Copy to put the result on your clipboard. A message appears only when something was actually copied.

A worked example

The form opens with these values: Wake-up time = 07:00; Minutes to fall asleep = 15; Sleep cycles (×90 min) = 5. Press Calculate once to see how the output is laid out, then replace them with your own data and press again — the new result replaces the old one.

Frequently asked questions

Is what I type uploaded anywhere?

No. Sleep Cycle Calculator processes "Wake-up time", "Minutes to fall asleep" and "Sleep cycles (×90 min)" with JavaScript inside your browser. Your inputs and the result stay on your device; Utilixo keeps no copy of them.

Can I keep several results to compare?

No. Each press of Calculate rebuilds the result from the current inputs and overwrites the previous one. To compare runs, note the earlier result down yourself.

Anything specific to watch with Sleep Cycle Calculator?

A sleep cycle averages about 90 minutes. Waking at the end of a cycle usually feels easier than waking mid-cycle.

How do I take the result away?

Press Copy after a result appears. The copy button only reports success when there is content to copy.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. Sleep Cycle Calculator is free, needs no registration and has no usage limit. Open the page and use it.
