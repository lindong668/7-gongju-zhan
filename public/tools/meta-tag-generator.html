About Meta Tag Generator

Build SEO, Open Graph and Twitter card tags and copy ready-to-paste HTML. Press Generate to see the result in the results panel. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Only the current result is shown — no history or side-by-side comparison.

Meta Tag Generator reads "Page title", "Description", "Canonical URL", "Image URL", "Site name" and "Twitter handle" from the form above and writes the answer into the results panel. There is no account, no upload and no server round-trip: the JavaScript on this page does the work on your own device.

How to use Meta Tag Generator

  1. Open Meta Tag Generator from Generators & Utilities, or find it with the search box in the header.
  2. Fill in the fields shown above: "Page title", "Description", "Canonical URL", "Image URL", "Site name" and "Twitter handle".
  3. Press Generate. The result replaces whatever was shown before; only the current run is displayed, never two results side by side.
  4. Use Copy HTML to put the result on your clipboard. A message appears only when something was actually copied.

A worked example

The form opens with these values: Page title = Percentage Calculator — Three Modes; Description = Calculate percentages in three modes, with the percent vs percentage-point distinction.; Canonical URL = https://example.com/percentage-calculator; Image URL = https://example.com/og-cover.png. Press Generate once to see how the output is laid out, then replace them with your own data and press again — the new result replaces the old one.

Frequently asked questions

Is what I type uploaded anywhere?

No. Meta Tag Generator processes "Page title", "Description", "Canonical URL", "Image URL", "Site name" and "Twitter handle" with JavaScript inside your browser. Your inputs and the result stay on your device; Utilixo keeps no copy of them.

Can I keep several results to compare?

No. Each press of Generate rebuilds the result from the current inputs and overwrites the previous one. To compare runs, note the earlier result down yourself.

Anything specific to watch with Meta Tag Generator?

The result depends only on the fields shown above ("Page title", "Description", "Canonical URL", "Image URL", "Site name" and "Twitter handle"); change one of them and press Generate again to get an updated answer.

How do I take the result away?

Press Copy HTML after a result appears. The copy button only reports success when there is content to copy.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. Meta Tag Generator is free, needs no registration and has no usage limit. Open the page and use it.
