About Slug Generator

Turn headlines into clean, URL-safe slugs with optional lowercasing and separator choice. Press Generate to see the result in the results panel. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Only the current result is shown — no history or side-by-side comparison.

Slug Generator reads "Headline or title", "Separator" and "Case" from the form above and writes the answer into the results panel. There is no account, no upload and no server round-trip: the JavaScript on this page does the work on your own device.

How to use Slug Generator

  1. Open Slug Generator from Text & Code, or find it with the search box in the header.
  2. Fill in the fields shown above: "Headline or title", "Separator" and "Case".
  3. Press Generate. The result replaces whatever was shown before; only the current run is displayed, never two results side by side.
  4. Use Copy to put the result on your clipboard. A message appears only when something was actually copied.

Frequently asked questions

Is what I type uploaded anywhere?

No. Slug Generator processes "Headline or title", "Separator" and "Case" with JavaScript inside your browser. Your inputs and the result stay on your device; Utilixo keeps no copy of them.

Can I keep several results to compare?

No. Each press of Generate rebuilds the result from the current inputs and overwrites the previous one. To compare runs, note the earlier result down yourself.

Anything specific to watch with Slug Generator?

The result depends only on the fields shown above ("Headline or title", "Separator" and "Case"); change one of them and press Generate again to get an updated answer.

How do I take the result away?

Press Copy after a result appears. The copy button only reports success when there is content to copy.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. Slug Generator is free, needs no registration and has no usage limit. Open the page and use it.
