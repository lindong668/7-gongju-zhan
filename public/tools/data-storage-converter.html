About Data Storage Converter

Convert bits and bytes with either decimal (1000) or binary (1024) bases. Press Convert to see the result in the results panel. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Only the current result is shown — no history or side-by-side comparison.

Data Storage Converter reads "Value", "From" and "Base" from the form above and writes the answer into the results panel — Drives are sold in decimal GB; Windows reports binary GiB. A "1 TB" drive shows as about 931 GB for that reason. There is no account, no upload and no server round-trip: the JavaScript on this page does the work on your own device.

How to use Data Storage Converter

  1. Open Data Storage Converter from Converters, or find it with the search box in the header.
  2. Fill in the fields shown above: "Value", "From" and "Base".
  3. Press Convert. The result replaces whatever was shown before; only the current run is displayed, never two results side by side.
  4. Use Copy to put the result on your clipboard. A message appears only when something was actually copied.

Frequently asked questions

Is what I type uploaded anywhere?

No. Data Storage Converter processes "Value", "From" and "Base" with JavaScript inside your browser. Your inputs and the result stay on your device; Utilixo keeps no copy of them.

Can I keep several results to compare?

No. Each press of Convert rebuilds the result from the current inputs and overwrites the previous one. To compare runs, note the earlier result down yourself.

Anything specific to watch with Data Storage Converter?

Drives are sold in decimal GB; Windows reports binary GiB. A "1 TB" drive shows as about 931 GB for that reason.

How do I take the result away?

Press Copy after a result appears. The copy button only reports success when there is content to copy.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. Data Storage Converter is free, needs no registration and has no usage limit. Open the page and use it.
