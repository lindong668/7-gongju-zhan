About Grade Calculator

Work out a weighted grade, or the score you still need on the final to hit a target. Press Calculate to see the result in the results panel. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Only the current result is shown — no history or side-by-side comparison.

Grade Calculator reads "Current grade (%)", "Target grade (%)" and "Final weight (%)" from the form above and writes the answer into the results panel. There is no account, no upload and no server round-trip: the JavaScript on this page does the work on your own device.

How to use Grade Calculator

  1. Open Grade Calculator from Health & Everyday, or find it with the search box in the header.
  2. Fill in the fields shown above: "Current grade (%)", "Target grade (%)" and "Final weight (%)".
  3. Press Calculate. The result replaces whatever was shown before; only the current run is displayed, never two results side by side.
  4. Use Copy to put the result on your clipboard. A message appears only when something was actually copied.

A worked example

The form opens with these values: Current grade (%) = 82; Target grade (%) = 90; Final weight (%) = 30. Press Calculate once to see how the output is laid out, then replace them with your own data and press again — the new result replaces the old one.

Frequently asked questions

Is what I type uploaded anywhere?

No. Grade Calculator processes "Current grade (%)", "Target grade (%)" and "Final weight (%)" with JavaScript inside your browser. Your inputs and the result stay on your device; Utilixo keeps no copy of them.

Can I keep several results to compare?

No. Each press of Calculate rebuilds the result from the current inputs and overwrites the previous one. To compare runs, note the earlier result down yourself.

Anything specific to watch with Grade Calculator?

The result depends only on the fields shown above ("Current grade (%)", "Target grade (%)" and "Final weight (%)"); change one of them and press Calculate again to get an updated answer.

How do I take the result away?

Press Copy after a result appears. The copy button only reports success when there is content to copy.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. Grade Calculator is free, needs no registration and has no usage limit. Open the page and use it.
