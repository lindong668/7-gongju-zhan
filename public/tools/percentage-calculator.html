About Percentage Calculator

Three modes: X% of Y, X is what percent of Y, and percentage change between two numbers. Press Calculate to see the result in the results panel. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Only the current result is shown — no history or side-by-side comparison.

Percentage Calculator reads "X (%)" and "Y" from the form above and writes the answer into the results panel — A percentage point is an absolute gap between two percentages; percent change is relative. 4% → 6% is 2 points but a 50% increase. There is no account, no upload and no server round-trip: the JavaScript on this page does the work on your own device.

How to use Percentage Calculator

  1. Open Percentage Calculator from Finance, or find it with the search box in the header.
  2. Fill in the fields shown above: "X (%)" and "Y".
  3. Press Calculate. The result replaces whatever was shown before; only the current run is displayed, never two results side by side.
  4. Use Copy to put the result on your clipboard. A message appears only when something was actually copied.

Frequently asked questions

Is what I type uploaded anywhere?

No. Percentage Calculator processes "X (%)" and "Y" with JavaScript inside your browser. Your inputs and the result stay on your device; Utilixo keeps no copy of them.

Can I keep several results to compare?

No. Each press of Calculate rebuilds the result from the current inputs and overwrites the previous one. To compare runs, note the earlier result down yourself.

Anything specific to watch with Percentage Calculator?

A percentage point is an absolute gap between two percentages; percent change is relative. 4% → 6% is 2 points but a 50% increase.

How do I take the result away?

Press Copy after a result appears. The copy button only reports success when there is content to copy.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. Percentage Calculator is free, needs no registration and has no usage limit. Open the page and use it.
