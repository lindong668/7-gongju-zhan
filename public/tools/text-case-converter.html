About Text Case Converter

Convert between UPPER, lower, Title Case, camelCase, snake_case, kebab-case and more. Press Convert to see the result in the results panel. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Only the current result is shown — no history or side-by-side comparison.

Text Case Converter reads "Your text" and "Convert to" from the form above and writes the answer into the results panel. There is no account, no upload and no server round-trip: the JavaScript on this page does the work on your own device.

How to use Text Case Converter

  1. Open Text Case Converter from Text & Code, or find it with the search box in the header.
  2. Fill in the fields shown above: "Your text" and "Convert to".
  3. Press Convert. The result replaces whatever was shown before; only the current run is displayed, never two results side by side.
  4. Use Copy to put the result on your clipboard. A message appears only when something was actually copied.

A worked example

Enter your own values in the fields above and press Convert. Change any input and press again; the result is recalculated from the current inputs and replaces the previous one.

Frequently asked questions

Is what I type uploaded anywhere?

No. Text Case Converter processes "Your text" and "Convert to" with JavaScript inside your browser. Your inputs and the result stay on your device; Utilixo keeps no copy of them.

Can I keep several results to compare?

No. Each press of Convert rebuilds the result from the current inputs and overwrites the previous one. To compare runs, note the earlier result down yourself.

Anything specific to watch with Text Case Converter?

The result depends only on the fields shown above ("Your text" and "Convert to"); change one of them and press Convert again to get an updated answer.

How do I take the result away?

Press Copy after a result appears. The copy button only reports success when there is content to copy.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. Text Case Converter is free, needs no registration and has no usage limit. Open the page and use it.
