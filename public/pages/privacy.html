Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

The short version

Yarrow Tools is built so that your data does not reach us. Every tool on this site performs its calculation locally in your web browser. Your text, numbers and files are not uploaded to a server, not stored in a database, and not shared with anyone.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect the text you paste into a tool.
  • We do not collect the numbers you enter into a calculator.
  • We do not upload images you select — they are read into the page and processed on your device.
  • We do not require an account, so we hold no names, emails or passwords.

What is stored on your device

We use local storage for one thing only: remembering whether you prefer the light or dark theme. This stays in your browser and is never transmitted.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertising served by third-party ad networks. Those providers may set cookies or use similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including interest-based advertising. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies, not ours. You can manage ad personalisation through your browser settings or the relevant industry opt-out tools.

Server logs

Like virtually every website, the hosting provider that serves these pages may record standard technical information such as IP address, user agent and the URL requested, for security and reliability. This is not linked to the content of what you calculate.

Children

These tools are general-purpose and not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Questions

Use the contact page.
