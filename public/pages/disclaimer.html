Estimates, not advice

Every calculator on this site applies a published formula to the numbers you enter. The output is an estimate. It is not financial advice, medical advice, tax advice, legal advice or investment advice, and it is not a substitute for consulting a qualified professional.

Why results differ from real life

  • Financial tools assume fixed rates and regular payments. Real loans carry fees, rate changes, insurance and taxes that a simple amortization model does not capture.
  • Health tools such as BMI, BMR and calorie estimates use population averages. Individual bodies vary considerably, and BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat.
  • Converters use standard factors, but local definitions (a US gallon versus an imperial gallon, for example) can differ from what a specific industry assumes.
  • Random generators use cryptographic randomness where available; the animation is presentation only.

Verify before you act

If a number matters — a mortgage, a tax filing, a medical decision — confirm it with the lender, the authority or the professional who is actually responsible for it.
