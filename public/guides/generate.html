Complete guide to Generators

A practical guide to the 12 Generators tools on Utilixo: what each one does, when to use them, and how your data stays on your device.

This guide covers the 12 utilities in the Generators category on Utilixo. Each one is a small, focused page that does a single job — CSS Box-Shadow Generator, Coin Flip, Color Picker, Dice Roller — without asking you to sign up or send anything to a server.

Every tool on this site runs locally in your browser. The moment you open a page, the code is already on your device; the numbers or text you type are processed there and are discarded as soon as you close the tab. That makes these pages fast, but it also means they keep working when your connection is slow or unavailable.

What is in this category

  • CSS Box-Shadow Generator — Build layered CSS box-shadows with a live preview and copy-ready code.
  • Coin Flip — Flip a coin once or many times, with a running tally of heads and tails.
  • Color Picker — Pick any color and read off HEX, RGB, HSL, plus contrast against black and white.
  • Dice Roller — Roll any number of dice with any number of sides, and see the total and breakdown.
  • Favicon Generator — Turn a square image into favicon sizes plus the HTML tags to install them.
  • Image Compressor — Shrink JPEG, PNG or WebP by quality and max width, entirely in your browser.
  • Lorem Ipsum Generator — Generate placeholder text by paragraph, sentence or word — classic Latin or plain English.
  • Meta Tag Generator — Build SEO, Open Graph and Twitter card tags and copy ready-to-paste HTML.
  • Password Generator — Generate strong passwords locally with crypto-grade randomness and no ambiguous characters option.
  • QR Code Generator — Turn any text or link into a QR code, rendered locally and downloadable as PNG.
  • Random Name Picker Wheel — Spin a wheel to pick a name fairly. Enter any list, watch it spin, remove winners and keep going.
  • UUID Generator — Generate RFC 4122 version 4 UUIDs, one at a time or in bulk.

When these tools help

The Generators category has 12 focused tools, including CSS Box-Shadow Generator, Coin Flip and Color Picker. Each page shows its own input fields and one action button; the result appears only after you press it.

Change an input and press the button again: the new result replaces the previous one and replaces it in the same results panel. Where a page has a Copy button, you can take the value straight to your clipboard.

Why everything runs on your device

A Generators calculation that sent your inputs to a server would leave a copy somewhere else. Here the JavaScript on each page does the work on your own device, so nothing you type is stored. The formulas and conversion factors live in the page itself, so the same input always gives the same result; where a method has limits such as rounding, regional conventions or financial assumptions, the tool page states them.

Common questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no registration, no email address and no quota. Open any tool and use it as often as you like, free of charge.

Are the results accurate enough for professional work?

The tools use standard formulas and official conversion factors, and each page states which method it applies. They are intended for everyday calculations and quick checks; for regulated financial, medical or legal decisions, always confirm with a qualified professional.

Can I use the output elsewhere?

Yes. Most tools offer a copy button that places the result on your clipboard and confirms with an on-screen message. Generator tools may offer a download instead. Nothing is watermarked or locked.
