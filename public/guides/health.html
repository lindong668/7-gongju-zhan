Complete guide to Health & Everyday

A practical guide to the 8 Health & Everyday tools on Utilixo: what each one does, when to use them, and how your data stays on your device.

This guide covers the 8 utilities in the Health & Everyday category on Utilixo. Each one is a small, focused page that does a single job — Age Calculator, BMI Calculator, Calorie & TDEE Calculator, Date Difference Calculator — without asking you to sign up or send anything to a server.

Every tool on this site runs locally in your browser. The moment you open a page, the code is already on your device; the numbers or text you type are processed there and are discarded as soon as you close the tab. That makes these pages fast, but it also means they keep working when your connection is slow or unavailable.

What is in this category

  • Age Calculator — Your exact age in years, months and days, plus totals down to the minute.
  • BMI Calculator — Body mass index in metric or imperial, with the WHO category and a healthy weight range.
  • Calorie & TDEE Calculator — BMR with Mifflin-St Jeor, then daily calories to maintain, lose or gain weight.
  • Date Difference Calculator — How many days, weeks, months and years between two dates — inclusive or exclusive.
  • Pregnancy Due Date Calculator — Estimate a due date from the last period, date of conception or a known ultrasound date.
  • GPA Calculator — Weighted and cumulative GPA on the standard 4.0 scale, with credit hours.
  • Grade Calculator — Work out a weighted grade, or the score you still need on the final to hit a target.
  • Sleep Cycle Calculator — Bedtimes and wake times aligned to 90-minute sleep cycles, with the 15-minute fall-asleep buffer.

When these tools help

The Health & Everyday category has 8 focused tools, including Age Calculator, BMI Calculator and Calorie & TDEE Calculator. Each page shows its own input fields and one action button; the result appears only after you press it.

Change an input and press the button again: the new result replaces the previous one and replaces it in the same results panel. Where a page has a Copy button, you can take the value straight to your clipboard.

Why everything runs on your device

A Health & Everyday calculation that sent your inputs to a server would leave a copy somewhere else. Here the JavaScript on each page does the work on your own device, so nothing you type is stored. The formulas and conversion factors live in the page itself, so the same input always gives the same result; where a method has limits such as rounding, regional conventions or financial assumptions, the tool page states them.

Common questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no registration, no email address and no quota. Open any tool and use it as often as you like, free of charge.

Are the results accurate enough for professional work?

The tools use standard formulas and official conversion factors, and each page states which method it applies. They are intended for everyday calculations and quick checks; for regulated financial, medical or legal decisions, always confirm with a qualified professional.

Can I use the output elsewhere?

Yes. Most tools offer a copy button that places the result on your clipboard and confirms with an on-screen message. Generator tools may offer a download instead. Nothing is watermarked or locked.
